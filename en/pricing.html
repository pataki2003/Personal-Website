When do I pay by the hour?

Small changes and bug fixes cost 15,000 Ft/hour. A standalone small assignment has a 30,000 Ft minimum fee. If investigation time is uncertain, we agree a time budget before starting.

When do I get a fixed price?

For a website, magazine, automation, or CRM, I prepare a written proposal after understanding the task. It lists working features at handover, materials needed from you, revision rounds, and the payment schedule. If the scope grows, we agree any extra work in advance.

When does an assessment cost money?

The first conversation is free. If a reliable proposal requires a detailed review of access, data, or existing processes, a separate 79,000 Ft assessment may be agreed. It produces a usable plan and itemized proposal. The proposal states whether this fee is credited toward development.

What happens after handover?

Occasional help is billed hourly, or you can choose care from, for example, 39,000 Ft/month, including monitoring, backup checks, and one hour of help per month. Hosting, licenses, and new features are agreed separately.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

Small changes are billed at 15,000 Ft per hour, with a 30,000 Ft minimum for a standalone task. For a new website, I provide a fixed project quote once the scope is clear.

What if the scope changes during the project?

We discuss new features or significant additions that were outside the original proposal before I build them. Any extra cost is agreed in advance.

Is the first conversation free?

Yes. The first conversation is free. A detailed assessment costs 79,000 Ft only when an existing system, data set, or process needs a closer review before I can quote accurately.

How does payment work?

Small hourly tasks are billed for the work completed. Fixed-price projects are paid in instalments tied to agreed milestones. The proposal always sets out the exact schedule.

How long does a project take?

A simple website may take a few weeks; a more involved web app or CRM can take longer. I confirm the timeline in the proposal after defining the scope.

What if I am not sure what I need yet?

That is fine. The first conversation is meant to identify the right solution and avoid building things you do not need.

Can you improve an existing system?

Yes, when keeping the existing foundation makes technical and business sense. I first review the current system and its constraints.

What happens after handover?

We can arrange hourly help or a separate monthly maintenance agreement if needed. New features, licences, and third-party services are priced separately.
