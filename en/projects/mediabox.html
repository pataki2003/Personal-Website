Projects / Business website and internal CRM

MEDIABOX.

MEDIABOX combines two connected systems: a public site explaining pizza-box advertising and an internal CRM for the sales workflow.

Status
Completed website and internal-use CRM
My role
Built the website and CRM
01 / The problem

The problem

Advertisers and pizzeria partners need different explanations of the same service. Meanwhile, leads, calls, tasks, and follow-ups need a structured place in the sales process.

02 / What I built

What I built

I built a business site for both audiences and an internal sales CRM. The CRM keeps contacts and next actions in one workflow.

03 / How it works

How it works

Partner / lead → customer record → activity / call → task → status → follow-up

04 / Key features

Key features

  • Distinct paths for advertisers and pizzerias
  • Customer records and sales activity
  • Tasks, statuses, and follow-ups
05 / My role

My role

I built the public website and internal CRM for the business. Sales records stay in the internal system; no real customer data is shown here.

06 / Technology

Technology

The public case material does not specify the website and CRM technology stack.

07 / Result / current status

Result / current status

Both interfaces have been built; the CRM is for internal use. No business performance figures are published.
