Projects / Study product

Studoid.

Studoid brings a student’s own courses, materials, and study time into one clear preparation flow. Its closed beta supports steady study and exam preparation.

Status
Closed beta; reading, notes, and personalization are being refined
My role
Product direction, requirements, UX decisions, and development coordination
studoid.hu ↗
01 / The problem

The problem

Course materials, deadlines, and available study time often live in separate places. That makes it hard to see what comes next or whether preparation is on track.

02 / What I built

What I built

Students can organize courses and their own source material, plan the week, then work through focused reading, recall, and exam practice.

03 / How it works

How it works

Course → own materials → weekly plan → reading / focus → recall → practice / exam mode

04 / Key features

Key features

  • Courses and personal materials
  • Weekly study plan and focus
  • Recall and exam mode
05 / My role

My role

I shape the product direction, requirements, and key UX decisions; coordinate development; and use an AI-assisted development workflow.

06 / Technology

Technology

The current public material does not specify the closed beta’s technology stack.

07 / Result / current status

Result / current status

The described study flow is part of the closed beta. Reading, note-taking, and personalization remain active areas of development.
