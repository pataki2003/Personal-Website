Projects / Food magazine

Gastro House.

Gastro House is a food magazine where articles can also become structured, printable recipes. Editing happens in the shared backend.

Status
A publication on the shared editorial system
My role
Magazine interface and recipe workflow
01 / The problem

The problem

A recipe needs more than a normal article layout: ingredients and steps must remain easy to follow on screen and on paper.

02 / What I built

What I built

Editors can switch an article into recipe mode, enter ingredients and steps separately, and publish a printable recipe for readers.

03 / How it works

How it works

Article → recipe mode → ingredients → steps → publish → printable recipe

04 / Key features

Key features

  • Food articles and categories
  • Structured ingredients and steps
  • Printable recipe view
05 / My role

My role

I shaped the magazine interface and fitted the recipe feature into the shared editorial workflow.

06 / Technology

Technology

Next.js and Payload CMS in the shared editorial system.
